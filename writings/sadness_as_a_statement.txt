title: Sadness as a statement
date: 11/25
type: philo, poem
lang: eng
description: To the cosmos: "I have not forgiven you"

---

Am I sad because I <i>want</i> to be sad? 

At this point, this feeling has been lasting so long that it cannot be a discrete contextual response.\\
Perhaps it is a choice, some kind of commitment.\\
Perhaps I believe being sad is the appropriate response to the current state of my life.\\
A self-perpetuated stance of disapproval I insist on wearing everyday as I parade around, in the hope that the cruel universe that orchestrated all of this realizes just how unjust it has been.\\
A statement to the cosmos: <i>"I have not forgiven you."</i>\\

Perhaps I am a toddler putting on an exagerated frown and crying wildly every time I get into mommy's field of view, to let her know just how much I disagree with the sentence, hoping to earn a few pitiful words of comfort for something I don't quite understand.\\ 
My child mind cannot comprehend the existential <i>why</i>.\\
It can barely grasp the phenomenological <i>why</i>, which we call the <i>how</i>.\\

<i>Are all emotions unreserved displays of approval and disapproval, directed towards a parental universe in the hope to spark a comforting motherly reaction?</i>

I am torn between gazing into this sadness and ignoring it to go on with my day.\\
I keep switching sides every couple of months, because none of these methods seem to dampen the sadness in any significant amount.\\
I am aware that my feelings shouldn't be ignored, but over years of looking at them without any change, there is a point where observation becomes devotion.\\
Acknowledging the sadness consistently is like keeping the flame lit. \\
Perhaps contemplating the sadness does not give it the acknowledgement it requires.\\
Perhaps the debt I owe this sadness cannot be paid with thoughts alone.\\
Perhaps this sadness requires me to live it wildly, to cry it out, to scream it at the top of my lungs, to shape it into yet another ungodly amount of pitiful songs and poems to be sung in the moonlight for everyone to hear.\\

I never could scream. Maybe that's what's holding me back.
